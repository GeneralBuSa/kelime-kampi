# conditionals-2|B2|Third and mixed conditionals|Koşul cümleleri: 3. tip ve karma tip
> 3. tip: geçmişte gerçekleşmemiş bir koşul ve sonucu (pişmanlık, eleştiri). If + past perfect, would have + V3.
> Karma tip: geçmişteki koşulun şimdiki sonucu. If + past perfect, would + V1 (If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now).
= If + had + V3, would have + V3 · If + had + V3, would + V1 (now)
e If you had told me, I would have helped you. || Bana söyleseydin sana yardım ederdim.
e If I had saved money, I would be rich now. || Para biriktirseydim şimdi zengin olurdum.
? If she had left earlier, she ___ the train.|would have caught|would catch;will catch;had caught|3. tip: would have + V3.
? If I ___ harder, I would have passed the exam.|had studied|studied;have studied;would study|3. tip if cümlesi: had + V3.
? If he had taken the medicine, he ___ better now.|would feel|would have felt;will feel;felt|Geçmişteki koşul, şimdiki sonuç ("now"): karma tip, would + V1.
? We would have won the match if our best player ___ injured.|hadn't been|wasn't;wouldn't be;hasn't been|3. tip if cümlesi: hadn't been.
? If I had accepted that job, I ___ in Germany now.|would be living|would have lived;will live;had lived|Karma tip: şu anki hayali durum ("now").

# past-perfect|B2|Past perfect and past perfect continuous|Past Perfect ve Past Perfect Continuous
> Geçmişteki iki olaydan önce olanı göstermek için past perfect (had + V3) kullanılır. "by the time, before, after, when, already" ile sık görülür.
> Geçmişteki bir andan önce bir süre devam etmiş eylemi vurgulamak için past perfect continuous (had been + -ing) kullanılır.
= had + V3 · had been + V-ing
e When we arrived, the film had already started. || Vardığımızda film çoktan başlamıştı.
e She was tired because she had been running. || Koştuğu için yorgundu.
? By the time the police arrived, the thief ___.|had escaped|escaped;has escaped;was escaping|Polis gelmeden önce tamamlanmış eylem: had escaped.
? I ___ that film before, so I didn't want to see it again.|had seen|have seen;saw;was seeing|Geçmişteki karardan önceki deneyim: had seen.
? Her eyes were red because she ___ for hours.|had been crying|has been crying;cried;was cried|Geçmişteki bir andan önce süren eylem: had been crying.
? After he ___ his homework, he went out.|had finished|has finished;was finishing;finishes|"After" ile önce olan eylem: had finished.
? When I got to the station, the train ___ already.|had left|has left;was leaving;leaves|İstasyona varmadan önce tamamlanmış: had left.

# future-advanced|B2|Future continuous and future perfect|Future Continuous ve Future Perfect
> Future continuous (will be + -ing): gelecekte belirli bir anda devam ediyor olacak eylem.
> Future perfect (will have + V3): gelecekte belirli bir zamandan önce tamamlanmış olacak eylem. "by + zaman" ile sık kullanılır.
= will be + V-ing · will have + V3
e This time tomorrow I will be flying to Rome. || Yarın bu saatte Roma'ya uçuyor olacağım.
e By 2030, they will have finished the project. || 2030'a kadar projeyi bitirmiş olacaklar.
? Don't call me at 8; I ___ dinner then.|will be having|will have had;have;am had|O anda devam ediyor olacak: will be having.
? By next June, I ___ from university.|will have graduated|will graduate;graduate;am graduating|"By next June" öncesinde tamamlanmış olacak: will have graduated.
? This time next week, we ___ on a beach in Antalya.|will be lying|will have lain;lie;are lain|Gelecekte belirli bir anda süren durum: will be lying.
? By the time you arrive, I ___ the cake.|will have baked|will bake;bake;baked|Gelmenden önce tamamlanmış olacak: will have baked.
? The scientists ___ the results by the end of the year.|will have published|will be publish;have published;publish|"by the end of the year" + tamamlanma: will have published.

# passive-2|B2|Advanced passive (modals, perfect, reporting verbs)|İleri edilgen yapılar (modal, perfect, it is said that)
> Modal ile edilgen: modal + be + V3 (must be done). Perfect ile: have/has been + V3. Devam eden: is being + V3.
> Haber/akademik dilde: It is said / believed / thought that ... ya da Özne + is said to + V1 / to have + V3 (geçmiş için).
= can/must/should be + V3 · has been + V3 · is being + V3 · is said to have + V3
e The work must be finished by Friday. || İş cuma gününe kadar bitirilmeli.
e He is believed to have left the country. || Ülkeyi terk ettiğine inanılıyor.
? The road ___ at the moment, so we have to use another route.|is being repaired|is repaired;has repaired;repairs|Şu an devam eden edilgen eylem: is being repaired.
? All the forms must ___ before Monday.|be completed|complete;completed;being completed|Modal + be + V3.
? The new hospital ___ recently.|has been opened|has opened;is opening;was opening|"recently" + edilgen perfect: has been opened.
? The painting is thought ___ in the 16th century.|to have been painted|to paint;to be painting;painting|Geçmişe ait bir durum: is thought to have been + V3.
? It ___ that the economy will grow next year.|is expected|expects;is expecting;has expected|"It is expected that..." edilgen kalıbı.

# causative|B2|Causative: have / get something done|Ettirgen yapı: have/get something done
> Bir işi başkasına yaptırdığımızı anlatır. Yapı: have/get + nesne + V3. Ayrıca "have someone do" ve "get someone to do" kalıpları da vardır.
= have + nesne + V3 · get + nesne + V3 · have sb do · get sb to do
e I had my hair cut yesterday. || Dün saçımı kestirdim.
e She got her car repaired. || Arabasını tamir ettirdi.
? I'm going to have my eyes ___ tomorrow.|tested|test;testing;to test|have + nesne + V3: tested.
? We had the house ___ last month.|painted|paint;painting;to paint|have + nesne + V3.
? I'll get the mechanic ___ the brakes.|to check|check;checked;checking|get + kişi + to + V1.
? She had her assistant ___ the documents.|send|to send;sent;sending|have + kişi + V1 (to almaz).
? You should get your phone ___; the screen is broken.|fixed|fix;to fix;fixing|get + nesne + V3.

# wish|B2|Wish and if only|Wish ve if only (dilek ve pişmanlık)
> Şimdiki durum için dilek: wish + past simple (I wish I were taller). Geçmiş için pişmanlık: wish + past perfect (I wish I had studied). Başkasının davranışından şikâyet: wish + would.
= wish + V2 (şimdi) · wish + had + V3 (geçmiş) · wish + sb + would + V1
e I wish I knew the answer. || Keşke cevabı bilsem.
e If only I hadn't said that. || Keşke bunu söylemeseydim.
? I wish I ___ a car. I'm tired of taking the bus.|had|have;would have;will have|Şimdiki durum için dilek: past simple.
? She wishes she ___ to the party last night.|had gone|went;would go;has gone|Geçmişle ilgili pişmanlık: had + V3.
? I wish you ___ making that noise! It's annoying.|would stop|stopped;stop;had stopped|Başkasının davranışından şikâyet: would + V1.
? If only I ___ more time to finish the project.|had|have;will have;would had|Şimdiki dilek: past simple.
? I wish I ___ so much yesterday. I feel sick now.|hadn't eaten|didn't eat;wouldn't eat;haven't eaten|Geçmişteki eylemden pişmanlık: hadn't eaten.

# past-modals|B2|Modals in the past (should have, must have, could have)|Geçmişe yönelik modallar
> should have + V3: yapılması gerekirdi ama yapılmadı (eleştiri/pişmanlık). must have + V3: geçmiş hakkında güçlü çıkarım. can't/couldn't have + V3: geçmişte olmuş olamaz. could/might/may have + V3: olmuş olabilir. needn't have + V3: gereksiz yere yapıldı.
= modal + have + V3
e You should have told me earlier. || Bana daha önce söylemeliydin.
e She must have forgotten our meeting. || Toplantımızı unutmuş olmalı.
? The ground is wet. It ___ last night.|must have rained|should have rained;can't have rained;must rain|Kanıta dayanan geçmiş çıkarım: must have rained.
? You failed the exam. You ___ harder.|should have studied|must have studied;could study;should study|Geçmişe yönelik eleştiri: should have + V3.
? He ___ the money; he was with me all day.|can't have stolen|must have stolen;should have stolen;needn't have stolen|Geçmişte imkânsız: can't have + V3.
? I'm not sure why she's late. She ___ the bus.|might have missed|must miss;should have missed;can't miss|Emin olunmayan geçmiş olasılık: might have + V3.
? We ___ so much food; only three guests came.|needn't have cooked|mustn't have cooked;can't have cooked;shouldn't cook|Gereksiz yere yapılmış iş: needn't have + V3.

# reported-2|B2|Reported questions and commands|Dolaylı sorular ve emirler
> Dolaylı soruda soru düzeni bozulur, düz cümle düzeni gelir (yardımcı fiil öznenin arkasına geçer). Evet/hayır soruları if/whether ile aktarılır.
> Emir ve ricalar: tell/ask + kişi + (not) to + V1.
= She asked me where I lived. · He asked if I was ready. · He told me to sit down.
e "Where do you work?" → She asked me where I worked. || Nerede çalıştığımı sordu.
e "Don't touch it!" → He told me not to touch it. || Ona dokunmamamı söyledi.
? She asked me where ___.|I lived|did I live;do I live;I do live|Dolaylı soruda düz cümle düzeni: where I lived.
? He asked me ___ I could speak French.|if|that;what;which|Evet/hayır sorusu: if/whether.
? The teacher told us ___ talk during the exam.|not to|don't;to not to;not|Olumsuz emir: told us not to + V1.
? I wonder what time ___.|the meeting starts|does the meeting start;starts the meeting;is the meeting start|"I wonder" sonrası düz cümle düzeni gelir.
? My mother asked me ___ the dishes.|to wash|wash;washing;that wash|ask + kişi + to + V1.

# relative-2|B2|Non-defining relative clauses and prepositions|Tanımlamayan ilgi cümleleri ve edatlı yapılar
> Tanımlamayan ilgi cümleleri ek bilgi verir ve virgülle ayrılır; bu cümlelerde "that" kullanılamaz.
> Edatlı yapılarda edat başa gelebilir: in which, to whom, of which. Bütün bir cümleye gönderme yaparken ", which" kullanılır.
= My brother, who lives in Izmir, ... · the house in which I grew up · He passed, which surprised everyone.
e Istanbul, which is the largest city in Turkey, has two continents. || Türkiye'nin en büyük şehri olan İstanbul iki kıtaya yayılır.
e The person to whom I spoke was helpful. || Konuştuğum kişi yardımcı oldu.
? My father, ___ is 60, still works every day.|who|that;which;whom|Virgüllü (tanımlamayan) cümlede "that" kullanılmaz; insan → who.
? He didn't call me back, ___ made me angry.|which|that;what;who|Bütün cümleye gönderme: ", which".
? The company for ___ she works is very successful.|which|that;whom;who|Edattan sonra nesne için "which".
? The woman to ___ I sent the email never replied.|whom|who;which;that|Edattan sonra insan için "whom".
? The students, most of ___ were tired, went home early.|whom|who;them;which|"most of whom": insanlar için edatlı yapı.

# inversion|C1|Inversion after negative adverbials|Devrik yapı (Inversion)
> Olumsuz veya kısıtlayıcı bir zarf cümlenin başına geldiğinde, soru cümlesindeki gibi yardımcı fiil öznenin önüne geçer. Resmi ve akademik dilde vurgu için kullanılır.
> Kalıplar: Never / Rarely / Seldom + aux + özne · Not only ... but also · Hardly/Scarcely ... when · No sooner ... than · Only then / Only after · Not until · Under no circumstances.
= Never have I seen ... · No sooner had I arrived than ... · Not only did he ..., but he also ...
e Never have I seen such a beautiful view. || Hiç bu kadar güzel bir manzara görmemiştim.
e No sooner had we sat down than the phone rang. || Oturur oturmaz telefon çaldı.
? Rarely ___ such an honest politician.|do we meet|we meet;we do meet;meet we|Olumsuz zarf başa gelince devrik: do we meet.
? No sooner had he left ___ it started to rain.|than|when;then;that|"No sooner ... than" kalıbı.
? Not only ___ the exam, but she also got the highest score.|did she pass|she passed;she did pass;passed she|"Not only" sonrası devrik: did she pass.
? Hardly had I closed my eyes ___ the alarm went off.|when|than;then;that|"Hardly/Scarcely ... when" kalıbı.
? Under no circumstances ___ leave the building during the test.|should you|you should;you must;must|Devrik yapı: should you leave.

# cleft|C1|Cleft sentences and emphasis|Vurgu cümleleri (Cleft sentences)
> Cümlenin bir bölümünü öne çıkarmak için: It is/was ... that/who ... ve What ... is/was ... yapıları kullanılır. Olumlu cümlede vurgu için do/does/did + V1 kullanılabilir.
= It was Ali who broke the window. · What I need is a holiday. · I did tell you!
e It was in 1923 that the Republic was founded. || Cumhuriyetin kurulduğu yıl 1923'tü.
e What surprised me was his answer. || Beni şaşırtan şey onun cevabıydı.
? It was my sister ___ found the lost keys.|who|which;what;where|İnsan vurgulanıyor: It was ... who.
? ___ I really want is a quiet weekend.|What|That;Which;It|"What ... is" vurgu kalıbı.
? It was only after the meeting ___ I understood the problem.|that|which;when;what|"It was ... that" kalıbı.
? I ___ lock the door; I'm sure of it.|did|do;have;was|Geçmişte vurgu: did + V1.
? What the government should do ___ invest in education.|is|are;it is;that|"What ... is + V1" kalıbı.

# subjunctive|C1|The subjunctive and "it's time"|Subjunctive (istek kipi) ve it's (high) time
> suggest, recommend, insist, demand, propose ile ve "It is essential/vital/important that" ile sonraki cümlede fiil özneye bakmadan yalın halde gelir (should de kullanılabilir).
> "It's (high) time + özne + past simple": bir şeyin artık yapılması gerektiğini anlatır. "would rather + özne + past simple": başkası için tercih.
= suggest that he go · It is essential that she be · It's time we left · I'd rather you stayed
e The doctor recommended that he stop smoking. || Doktor sigarayı bırakmasını önerdi.
e It's high time you found a job. || Artık bir iş bulmanın zamanı geldi.
? The committee insisted that the report ___ before Friday.|be submitted|is submitted;was submitted;will be submitted|Subjunctive: fiil yalın halde (be submitted).
? It is vital that every student ___ the safety rules.|follow|follows;followed;will follow|"It is vital that" + yalın fiil.
? It's time we ___ home; it's very late.|went|go;will go;have gone|"It's time + özne + past simple".
? I'd rather you ___ smoke in the car.|didn't|don't;won't;not|"would rather + özne + past simple" (olumsuz: didn't).
? She suggested that we ___ a taxi.|take|took;will take;taking|"suggest that" + yalın fiil.

# participle|C1|Participle clauses|Sıfat-fiil ve zarf-fiil cümlecikleri (Participle clauses)
> Aynı özneye sahip iki cümle kısaltılabilir. Etken anlam → V-ing (Walking home, I saw...). Edilgen anlam → V3 (Built in 1900, the house...). Önce tamamlanmış eylem → Having + V3.
= V-ing ..., · V3 ..., · Having + V3 ..., · Having been + V3 ...,
e Having finished her work, she went home. || İşini bitirdikten sonra eve gitti.
e Written in simple English, the book is easy to read. || Sade bir İngilizceyle yazıldığı için kitap kolay okunuyor.
? ___ the email, I realised I had made a mistake.|Having sent|Sent;Being sent;Have sent|Önce tamamlanmış etken eylem: Having + V3.
? ___ in 1453, the city became the capital of the empire.|Conquered|Conquering;Having conquered;Conquer|Edilgen anlam (şehir fethedildi): V3.
? ___ along the beach, we saw a group of dolphins.|Walking|Walked;Having walked;To walk|Eş zamanlı etken eylem: V-ing.
? ___ by the noise, the baby started crying.|Frightened|Frightening;Having frightening;Frighten|Bebek korkutuldu (edilgen): V3.
? Not ___ what to say, he remained silent.|knowing|known;having know;to know|Olumsuz participle: Not + V-ing.

# conditionals-3|C1|Advanced conditionals (inverted, unless, but for)|İleri koşul yapıları
> "If" atılarak devrik koşul kurulabilir: Had I known (= If I had known), Were I you (= If I were you), Should you need (= If you need).
> Diğer bağlaçlar: unless (= if not), provided/providing that, as long as, on condition that, otherwise, but for / without (= olmasaydı).
= Had + özne + V3 · Were + özne · Should + özne + V1 · But for + isim
e Had I known about the problem, I would have helped. || Sorunu bilseydim yardım ederdim.
e Should you have any questions, please contact us. || Herhangi bir sorunuz olursa lütfen bize ulaşın.
? ___ I known you were ill, I would have visited you.|Had|If;Were;Should|Devrik 3. tip: Had I known.
? ___ you need further information, please call our office.|Should|Had;Were;Would|Devrik 1. tip: Should you need.
? You won't improve ___ you practise every day.|unless|if;provided;as long as|"unless" = eğer ... -mezsen.
? ___ your help, I would never have finished the project.|But for|Unless;Despite;In case|"But for" = (yardımın) olmasaydı.
? ___ I in your position, I would accept the offer.|Were|Was;Had;Should|Devrik 2. tip: Were I.

# ellipsis|C1|Substitution and ellipsis (so, neither, do so)|Yerine koyma ve eksiltme (so do I, neither, so / not)
> Tekrardan kaçınmak için: So + aux + özne (ben de), Neither/Nor + aux + özne (ben de ... -mem). "I think so / I hope not" kalıplarında "so/not" bir cümlenin yerini tutar.
= So do I · Neither can she · I hope so · I'm afraid not
e "I love jazz." "So do I." || "Cazı severim." "Ben de."
e "I can't swim." "Neither can I." || "Yüzemem." "Ben de yüzemem."
? "I've never been to Japan." "___ I."|Neither have|So have;Neither am;So am|Olumsuz cümleye katılma, present perfect: Neither have I.
? "She works in a bank." "So ___ her husband."|does|is;has;do|Geniş zamanda tekil özneyle: So does.
? "Will it rain tomorrow?" "I hope ___. The garden needs water."|so|not;it;yes|Olumlu beklenti: I hope so.
? "Is the shop open on Sunday?" "I'm afraid ___."|not|no;so;none|Olumsuz cevap kalıbı: I'm afraid not.
? He promised to help, and he did ___.|so|it;that;too|"did so" = söz verdiği şeyi yaptı.

# time-clauses|YDS|Tense agreement and time clauses|Zaman uyumu ve zaman bağlaçları (YDS)
> YDS'nin en sık sorduğu konudur. Zaman bağlaçlarından (when, as soon as, until, before, after, by the time, once) sonra gelecek anlamı için will kullanılmaz; present simple veya present perfect gelir.
> Geçmişte: by the time + past simple → ana cümle past perfect. Gelecekte: by the time + present simple → ana cümle future perfect. "since" + past simple → ana cümle present perfect.
= by the time + V2, had + V3 · by the time + V1, will have + V3 · since + V2, have + V3
e By the time the ambulance arrived, the patient had lost a lot of blood. || Ambulans geldiğinde hasta çok kan kaybetmişti.
e I will call you as soon as I arrive. || Varır varmaz seni arayacağım.
? By the time the rescue team reached the village, the floodwaters ___ much of the farmland.|had destroyed|have destroyed;destroy;will destroy|"by the time + past simple" → ana cümle past perfect.
? The results will be announced as soon as all the data ___ analysed.|has been|will be;would be;had been|Zaman bağlacından sonra will gelmez; present perfect edilgen: has been analysed.
? Since the industrial revolution began, global temperatures ___ steadily.|have risen|rose;had risen;are rising|"since + past simple" → ana cümle present perfect.
? By the time the new law comes into force, many companies ___ their policies.|will have changed|have changed;had changed;changed|Gelecekte "by the time + V1" → future perfect.
? The researchers did not publish their findings until they ___ the experiment several times.|had repeated|have repeated;repeat;will repeat|Geçmişte "until" ile önce tamamlanan eylem: had repeated.
? Once the committee ___ a decision, the results will be announced.|has made|will make;had made;makes|"once" zaman bağlacından sonra will gelmez; present perfect uygun.
? After she ___ the documents, she left the office.|had signed|has signed;signs;will sign|Geçmişte "after" ile önce biten eylem: past perfect.

# adverbial|YDS|Adverbial clauses: contrast, reason, result|Zarf cümlecikleri: zıtlık, sebep, sonuç (YDS)
> Zıtlık: although/though/even though + cümle; despite/in spite of + isim/V-ing; whereas/while = oysa (iki durumu karşılaştırır).
> Sebep: because/since/as + cümle; because of/due to/owing to + isim. Sonuç: so + sıfat + that; such + (a) + sıfat + isim + that; so that = -sın diye (amaç).
= although + clause · despite + noun · so ... that · such ... that · so that + can/could
e Despite the heavy rain, the match continued. || Şiddetli yağmura rağmen maç devam etti.
e The test was so difficult that nobody passed. || Sınav o kadar zordu ki kimse geçemedi.
? ___ the treatment is expensive, many patients prefer it because of its success rate.|Although|Despite;Because of;In spite of|Arkasından tam cümle var ve zıtlık bildiriyor: Although.
? ___ the lack of funding, the project was completed on time.|Despite|Although;Even though;Whereas|Arkasından isim öbeği geliyor: Despite.
? The new drug was ___ effective that doctors began prescribing it widely.|so|such;too;very|"so + sıfat + that".
? Some species thrive in cold climates, ___ others can only survive in warm regions.|whereas|because;so that;despite|İki durumu karşılaştırma: whereas (oysa).
? The flight was cancelled ___ the severe weather conditions.|due to|because;although;so that|Arkasından isim öbeği gelen sebep: due to.
? She saved money ___ she could study abroad.|so that|such that;because of;despite|Amaç: so that + could.
? ___ being expensive, the treatment is widely used.|In spite of|Although;Even though;Whereas|"In spite of" + V-ing / isim.
? It was ___ a difficult exam that almost nobody finished it.|such|so;too;very|"such a + sıfat + isim + that".

# conjunctions|YDS|Linkers and transitions|Bağlaçlar ve geçiş ifadeleri (YDS)
> Cümle başı geçiş ifadeleri noktalı virgül/noktadan sonra gelir ve virgül alır: However, Nevertheless, Moreover, Furthermore, Therefore, Consequently, In contrast, Likewise, Otherwise.
> Koşul bağlaçları: provided that / as long as (şartıyla), unless (-medikçe), in case (ihtimaline karşı), even if (-se bile). Anlam ilişkisini (zıtlık mı, ekleme mi, sonuç mu) önce tespit et.
= Zıtlık: however, nevertheless · Ekleme: moreover, furthermore · Sonuç: therefore, thus, consequently
e The method is cheap; however, it is not very accurate. || Yöntem ucuz; ancak çok doğru değil.
? Solar energy is clean and renewable; ___, it is not available at night.|however|moreover;therefore;likewise|İki cümle arasında zıtlık: however.
? The company lost its biggest client. ___, it had to lay off many workers.|Consequently|Nevertheless;However;In contrast|Sebep-sonuç ilişkisi: Consequently.
? Regular exercise strengthens the heart. ___, it reduces stress.|Moreover|However;Otherwise;Instead|Olumlu bir bilgiye ek: Moreover.
? You can borrow my car ___ you return it by Sunday.|provided that|unless;whereas;even though|Şart: provided that.
? Take an umbrella ___ it rains later.|in case|unless;so that;although|İhtimale karşı: in case.
? The patient will not recover ___ he receives proper treatment.|unless|if;provided that;as long as|"-medikçe": unless.
? The evidence is limited; ___, the hypothesis remains useful.|nevertheless|therefore;moreover;likewise|Zıtlık: nevertheless.
? Leave early; ___, you will miss the last train.|otherwise|however;moreover;therefore|Aksi halde: otherwise.

# noun-clauses|YDS|Noun clauses (that, whether, wh-, the fact that)|İsim cümlecikleri (Noun clauses)
> Bir cümle, başka bir cümlenin öznesi ya da nesnesi olabilir. that (... olduğunu), whether (... olup olmadığını), what/how/why (ne/nasıl/neden ... olduğunu), the fact that (... olduğu gerçeği).
> Wh- ile başlayan isim cümleciklerinde soru düzeni yoktur: I don't know where he lives (NOT where does he live).
= that + clause · whether (or not) · what/why/how + özne + fiil · the fact that
e What surprised scientists was how quickly the virus spread. || Bilim insanlarını şaşırtan şey virüsün ne kadar hızlı yayıldığıydı.
e It is not clear whether the drug is safe. || İlacın güvenli olup olmadığı belli değil.
? Researchers are still trying to understand ___ some people are more resistant to the disease.|why|that;what;whether or not|"neden ... olduğunu" anlamı: why.
? ___ the climate is changing is accepted by most scientists.|That|What;Whether;If|"İklimin değiştiği" (gerçek bir bilgi) özne: That.
? It is uncertain ___ the new policy will reduce unemployment or not.|whether|that;what;which|"or not" ile "olup olmadığı": whether.
? ___ he apologised did not change the committee's decision.|The fact that|What;Whether;That fact|"... olduğu gerçeği": The fact that.
? We need to find out ___ caused the system failure.|what|that;whether;which that|Neyin sebep olduğu (özne yok, what özne): what.
? I wonder ___ the results can be trusted.|whether|what;which;who|"olup olmadığı": whether.
? ___ remains unclear is how the virus entered the hospital.|What|That;Whether;Which|Cümlenin öznesi "ne olduğu": What remains unclear.

# reduced-relative|YDS|Reduced relative clauses|Kısaltılmış ilgi cümleleri (YDS)
> who/which + be kısaltılabilir: etken anlam → V-ing (the man standing there), edilgen anlam → V3 (the data collected in 2020). "the first/last/only + to + V1" kalıbı da sık sorulur.
= the people living here · the methods used · the first woman to win
e The questions asked in the interview were difficult. || Mülakatta sorulan sorular zordu.
e Marie Curie was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize. || Marie Curie Nobel kazanan ilk kadındı.
? The data ___ in the survey were analysed by experts.|collected|collecting;to collect;collects|Veriler toplandı (edilgen): collected.
? Students ___ in the dormitory must follow the rules.|living|lived;live;to living|Öğrenciler yaşıyor (etken): living.
? Neil Armstrong was the first person ___ on the moon.|to walk|walking;walked;who walk|"the first + to + V1".
? Most of the products ___ in this factory are exported.|manufactured|manufacturing;manufacture;to manufacturing|Ürünler üretiliyor (edilgen): manufactured.
? The number of people ___ from obesity has increased dramatically.|suffering|suffered;suffer;to suffered|İnsanlar acı çekiyor (etken): suffering.
? The only scientist ___ the award was from Sweden.|to receive|receiving;received;who receive|"the only + to + V1".
? Buildings ___ in the 19th century need careful restoration.|constructed|constructing;construct;to constructing|Binalar inşa edildi (edilgen): constructed.

# quantifiers-yds|YDS|Quantifiers and agreement|Miktar belirteçleri ve özne-fiil uyumu (YDS)
> few/a few + sayılabilen; little/a little + sayılamayan. "a" olmadan olumsuz anlam (çok az), "a" ile olumlu anlam (biraz).
> both ... and (çoğul fiil), either ... or / neither ... nor (fiil yakın özneye uyar). "The number of" + çoğul isim → tekil fiil; "A number of" + çoğul isim → çoğul fiil. each/every → tekil fiil.
= a number of + are · the number of + is · neither A nor B + (B'ye uyan fiil)
e A number of students were absent. || Birçok öğrenci yoktu.
e The number of students is increasing. || Öğrenci sayısı artıyor.
? The number of international students ___ risen sharply in recent years.|has|have;are;were|"The number of" tekil fiil alır: has.
? Very ___ people understood the lecture, so the professor explained it again.|few|little;a little;much|"people" sayılabilen, olumsuz anlam (çok az): few.
? Neither the manager nor the employees ___ aware of the problem.|were|was;is;has been|"neither ... nor" yakın özneye uyar: employees → were.
? There is ___ evidence to support this theory, so scientists remain sceptical.|little|few;a few;many|"evidence" sayılamaz, olumsuz anlam: little.
? Each of the participants ___ a questionnaire.|was given|were given;have given;are giving|"Each of" tekil fiil alır: was given.
? A number of studies ___ this conclusion.|support|supports;has supported;is supporting|"A number of" çoğul fiil: support.
? There is ___ time left, so we must hurry.|little|few;a few;many|"time" sayılamaz; olumsuz: little.

# prepositions-yds|YDS|Dependent prepositions|Fiil/sıfat + edat kalıpları (YDS)
> YDS'de kelime ile birlikte kullanılan edat sıkça sorulur: rely on, consist of, result in, result from, contribute to, account for, be aware of, be responsible for, be capable of, in terms of, in addition to, on behalf of.
= depend on · consist of · result in (sonuç) / result from (sebep) · contribute to · be exposed to
e The success of the project depends on funding. || Projenin başarısı finansmana bağlıdır.
? The committee consists ___ seven experts.|of|in;from;on|consist of = -den oluşmak.
? Air pollution can contribute ___ respiratory diseases.|to|in;for;on|contribute to = katkıda bulunmak / yol açmak.
? The accident resulted ___ the driver's carelessness.|from|in;to;of|result from = -den kaynaklanmak (sebep).
? Poor diet may result ___ serious health problems.|in|from;of;to|result in = ile sonuçlanmak (sonuç).
? Many developing countries rely heavily ___ agriculture.|on|in;of;to|rely on = -e dayanmak, güvenmek.
? Workers who are exposed ___ chemicals must wear protective clothing.|to|with;at;from|be exposed to = -e maruz kalmak.
? She spoke ___ behalf of the patients' association.|on|in;at;for|"on behalf of" = adına.
? The report is written ___ terms of cost, not quality.|in|on;at;for|"in terms of" = açısından.

# comparison-yds|YDS|Advanced comparison structures|İleri karşılaştırma yapıları (YDS)
> the + comparative ..., the + comparative (ne kadar ... o kadar). as + sıfat + as (kadar). twice/three times as ... as (iki/üç kat). not as/so ... as. far/much/considerably + comparative (çok daha).
= The more you read, the more you learn. · twice as large as · much more efficient than
e The earlier you start, the better your results will be. || Ne kadar erken başlarsan sonuçların o kadar iyi olur.
? The more you practise, the ___ you become.|more confident|most confident;confident;confidently|"The more ..., the more ..." kalıbı.
? This new engine is ___ more efficient than the old one.|much|very;so;too|Karşılaştırmayı güçlendirme: much more.
? The population of the city is twice as large ___ it was in 1990.|as|than;so;like|"twice as ... as" kalıbı.
? The problem is not ___ serious as the media suggests.|as|more;so much;such|"not as ... as".
? ___ the population grows, the greater the demand for water becomes.|The more|More;As more;The most|"The more ..., the greater ..." kalıbı.
? This method is far ___ than the previous one.|more reliable|reliable;most reliable;reliably|"far + comparative".
? The new lab is three times as large ___ the old one.|as|than;so;that|"three times as ... as".

# gerund-yds|YDS|Gerunds and infinitives in academic contexts|Gerund ve infinitive: akademik kullanımlar (YDS)
> -ing alanlar: avoid, consider, involve, risk, deny, postpone, be worth, it's no use, look forward to, object to, be committed to. (Son üçünde "to" bir edattır!)
> to + V1 alanlar: aim, manage, fail, tend, seem, attempt, be likely to, in order to. Anlam değiştirenler: stop doing (bırakmak) / stop to do (yapmak için durmak); remember doing / remember to do.
= look forward to doing · be committed to improving · tend to do · fail to do
e The government is committed to reducing poverty. || Hükümet yoksulluğu azaltmaya kararlıdır.
? Scientists have failed ___ a cure for the disease so far.|to find|finding;find;to finding|"fail" sonrası to + V1.
? The project involves ___ data from thousands of patients.|collecting|to collect;collect;collected|"involve" sonrası V-ing.
? We look forward to ___ your proposal.|receiving|receive;received;be receiving|"look forward to" — buradaki "to" edat, V-ing gelir.
? People tend ___ more when they are stressed.|to eat|eating;eat;to eating|"tend" sonrası to + V1.
? It is worth ___ that the study had a small sample size.|noting|to note;note;noted|"be worth" sonrası V-ing.
? He stopped ___ a cigarette, then continued walking.|to smoke|smoking;smoke;smoked|Sigara içmek için durdu (amaç): stop to do.
? The board objected to ___ the factory without a vote.|closing|close;to close;closed|"object to" — to edattır, V-ing gelir.
? Remember ___ the windows before you leave.|to lock|locking;lock;locked|Yapılacak işi hatırla: remember to do.
